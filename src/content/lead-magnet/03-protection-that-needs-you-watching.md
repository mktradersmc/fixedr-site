## Named

A rule like "close before high-impact news" or "stop at the daily limit" only holds if you're at
the screen, awake, and fast enough, at the exact second it matters. Protection that depends
entirely on your attention is protection for exactly as long as your attention lasts — not one
second longer.

## What actually fixes it

The response has to escalate: start with the human, and only act without one if there's genuinely
no response in time. The machine is the net underneath, never the first thing that moves.

## How the platform closes it

<!-- E-C136 -->
A warning first, then a louder alarm, and only if there is still no response within a short window,
an automatic close. The human gets the first chance, every time — the automatic step exists only
as the backstop under a trader who genuinely isn't there to answer.

## What it competes against

Watching the terminal yourself around news releases or a fast-moving account and closing manually
if it turns against you — which works exactly as reliably as your ability to be at the screen at
the right second, and no better.
