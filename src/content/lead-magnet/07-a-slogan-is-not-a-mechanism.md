## Named

"Plan your trade, trade your plan" hangs in every trading chat as a truth nobody disputes and
almost nobody needs reminding of — and it changes nothing at the exact moment risk is actually
taken. Agreement isn't the same as enforcement, and the gap between them is where the plan breaks.

## What actually fixes it

A mantra can't do what a structural condition does. The plan needs a path where breaking it simply
isn't available, not one more sentence to remember under pressure.

## How the platform closes it

<!-- E-C138 -->
The same enforced stop that fixes risk at order time (see Chapter 1) also makes "trade your plan"
a fact of how the interface works, not a promise you renew every trade — every protective feature
is worded and built as support, on purpose, because a mechanism doesn't need to be obeyed to hold.

## What it competes against

Repeating or reading a trading mantra picked up from a community — accurate, agreed on by
everyone, and, standing on its own, exactly as inert as a line on a wall.
