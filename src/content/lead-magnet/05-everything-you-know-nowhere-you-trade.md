## Named

You've read about journaling, you know your R-multiples, you can explain process over outcome to
anyone who asks. Then you sit down at the terminal you actually trade on, and none of it exists
there. Every session starts from zero, because the terminal only knows the order it's about to
place.

## What actually fixes it

The process has to live in the same place the trade happens — on the same machine, not split
between memory and terminal, and not stored somewhere you don't control either.

## How the platform closes it

<!-- E-C154 -->
Everything runs on your own machine, nothing on a server for other people's machines. What you
define, repeat, and log stays in the same place the trade is placed — not scattered between what
you remember and what the terminal shows.

## What it competes against

Trading on a plain execution terminal and keeping the journaling knowledge only in your head —
everything you've learned about your own process exists between your ears, and nowhere the
terminal you're actually clicking in can reach it.
