## Named

Following someone else's calls or mood in a group chat feels like orientation. It isn't a process
— it's a mood you didn't generate, standing in for one. When that source goes quiet, changes its
mind, or is simply having a bad week, there's nothing of your own left to fall back on.

## What actually fixes it

Support has to come from something that holds regardless of anyone's mood that day — including
your own. A mechanism, described honestly as a mechanism, not a person or a persona to trust.

## How the platform closes it

<!-- E-C138 -->
Protective features are built and worded to support the trader, never as a guarantor of an
outcome — the language stays a safety net under a process, not a promise standing in front of one.
What holds under pressure is the mechanism, not a call from someone else that day.

## What it competes against

Following the signals or mood of a discord or chat group for orientation — you inherit whatever
that source is feeling that day, including its dollar-sign thinking, in place of a process that's
actually yours.
