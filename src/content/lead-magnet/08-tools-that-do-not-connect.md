## Named

A charting platform here, an indicator there, a separate spreadsheet or journal somewhere else.
Every piece of the process technically exists — and nothing forces them into a sequence. Defining
a setup, repeating it, measuring it, and judging it stay four separate habits instead of one chain.

## What actually fixes it

The cycle needs to live in one place, on the machine the trade actually happens on, so nothing has
to be assembled by hand and nothing has to be exported from one tool and re-entered into another.

## How the platform closes it

<!-- E-C154 -->
One local environment holds the setup definition, the repeated trades, the measurement, and the
record together — on your own machine, where the trade is placed. Nothing to sync, nothing living
in a separate account somewhere else that has to be checked on its own.

## What it competes against

Running several separate tools in parallel — a charting platform, an indicator or two, a
spreadsheet or a standalone journal — each covering its own slice, with no forced chain connecting
definition to repetition to measurement to judgment.
