## Named

Buying a small risk-manager or lot-size script as a one-time file solves one calculation and opens
a new question nobody asks out loud: what happens to this file, and to you, if the person who
built it stops answering? A single purchase from a single anonymous seller carries no continuity
plan at all.

## What actually fixes it

Depending on one person's ongoing availability is a real risk, and it has to be answered with
architecture, not with a reassurance that person might not be around to keep.

## How the platform closes it

<!-- E-C134 -->
If a license check can't reach the server, the software keeps running rather than locking you
out. And if development stops for good, the last working version unlocks permanently instead of
quietly turning into a file that no longer works.

## What it competes against

Buying a finished risk-manager or lot-size calculator as a one-time file from the usual scattered
marketplace of scripts around prop trading — it does its one calculation, asks nothing back, and
carries no plan for what happens the day the person who wrote it moves on.
